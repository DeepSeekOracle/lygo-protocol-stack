LYGO TV disclaimer

Last updated: 5 September 2026 · Applies to https://chatagent.ca/sources/

1. This site is a portal only

LYGO TV is a browser catalog and player pointer. It is not a television station, cable company, IPTV reseller, or video host. We do not operate the third-party CDNs that deliver video. We do not store, transcode, decrypt, or proxy channel streams on chatagent.ca.

When you click a channel, your browser requests that stream (or official embed) directly from the publisher or list source. If a stream is geo-blocked, offline, CORS-blocked, or HTTP-only, that is a named miss. We do not invent a working channel.

2. Channels listed here are found free to watch online

Public lists (for example iptv-org, Free-TV, i.mjh.nz, Plex FAST catalogs, and GitHub M3U files) publish links to channels that those projects present as free to watch online (over-the-air, public web, or free ad-supported TV). The Channel tab embeds Excavationpro rooms on Kick, Rumble, Twitch, and YouTube using each platform’s official player.

chatagent.ca does not sell subscriptions, decrypt pay-TV, or provide pirate access. Availability, legality, and age-appropriateness in your region are determined by the original publisher and by the law where you watch. You are responsible for how you use third-party streams.

3. We do not control third-party content

Titles, logos, group names, and program content come from third-party playlists and platforms. Lists change without notice. A channel that was a public news feed yesterday may be dead or re-labeled today. We are not the broadcaster and cannot guarantee:

  • that a stream is live, accurate, or still public;
  • that a title matches what actually plays;
  • that a “Kids” or “18+” label is complete or correct;
  • that content is legal to watch in your country.

4. Kids listings

Channels marked Kids are labeled from public playlist metadata (category files such as iptv-org’s kids list, group-title, or words such as “kids” / “children” in the title). That is a hint, not a parental-control system and not a guarantee that the video is suitable for children. Ads, autoplay, and third-party players may still show unexpected material. Supervise minors. Do not treat LYGO TV as a child-safe app.

5. Adult / 18+ listings

Channels marked 18+ are flagged from public metadata (for example group titles or names containing adult / XXX / NSFW / 18+). 18+ listings are hidden by default and require an on-page confirmation that you are 18 or older before playback. Metadata can be wrong in both directions: some adult streams may be unlabeled, and some labeled streams may not be adult. If you are under 18, do not enable 18+ listings and do not play them.

We do not add a dedicated adult/XXX catalog file. Residual 18+ rows can still appear inside large mixed public lists. The Hide 18+ control and the confirm prompt are the hardening layer.

6. What you must not do

  • Do not use this portal to seek stolen, decrypted, or paywalled television;
  • Do not paste private, credentialed, or clearly infringing stream URLs;
  • Do not treat embeds as our content or as a license from the copyright owner.
