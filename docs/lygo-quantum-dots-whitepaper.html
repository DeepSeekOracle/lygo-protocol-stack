6. Materials honesty

CdSe/ZnS quantum dots are the textbook visible emitters. They are also a poor default for blood, brain, or long-term implant without heavy shielding — cadmium is toxic. A real-world path uses InP, carbon dots, silicon, or perovskite hosts designed for biocompatibility, NV-diamond for magnetometry (already a lab tool), and DNA origami only where chemistry is actually published. LYGO does not need a specific brand of nanocrystal. It needs a host that can fail closed.
