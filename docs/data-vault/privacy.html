What the Matrix Vault publishes, what it deliberately leaves out, and how the public reader handles sources.

Published

Full whitepapers, protocol and architecture documents, seal papers, lore, and selected public-interest LYGO documents. A paper is published in full when it is classified as a paper; it is not reduced to a teaser.

Grok exchanges

Grok material is published as curated excerpts only. The vault does not publish full chat transcripts, ordinary conversations, private messages, account data, or personal file contents.

Secrets and private material

The generation pass applies a secret scan for API keys, bot tokens, private keys, passwords, and similar credentials. Matches are redacted. Private dumps, normal chats, credentials, and personal files are excluded rather than published.

Source fingerprints

Each paper exposes a SHA-256 fingerprint so a reader can verify the public text against a copy. Source paths are shown as provenance labels; they are not links into a private filesystem.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense (publisher ca-pub-0646320966060599). Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies for details. The archive content itself is readable without an account.

Corrections

If a document should be removed or a redaction is incomplete, contact the operator through chatagent.ca. Removal requests are honored for private material and credentials.

This is a public archive of LYGO work. It is not a legal deposit, a complete backup, or a claim that every historical file is public. The published text is the cleaned public copy.
