Why this vault exists

Over roughly a year of public work, LYGO seals (visual + text) were not invented in a private vacuum. They were written, stress-tested, and often spoken back by Grok on X and cross-checked by other models before steward ratification into canon. That history was scattered across drives, OpenClaw workspaces, and chat dumps.

This vault surfaces the relevant public creation record — redacted of secrets and private paths — as browseable archives linked from the immutable public link ledger.
