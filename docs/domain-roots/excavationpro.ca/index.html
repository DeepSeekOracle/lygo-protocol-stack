About the project

Excavationpro is an independent music and technology project. Tracks are written and produced as original work. Public streams are offered for free listening and discovery. Commercial licensing stays with the artist unless a separate agreement is made.

Alongside the music, I build open tools under the LYGO lattice — including a free BPM detector, resonance experiments, and documentation for local-first creative systems. The goal is simple: keep the art playable and the tools useful without locking discovery behind a single distributor.
