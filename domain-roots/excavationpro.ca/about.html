About Excavationpro

Excavationpro is the music and creative-tech project of Justin Helmer (also Lightfather). The work spans original recordings, free public streams, and browser tools for producers.

Music

Releases explore hip-hop, experimental, and electronic forms. A large free listen catalog is available in the browser for discovery; platform links (Spotify, YouTube Music, and others) are provided when tracks are distributed there.

Tools

Public tools include a free BPM finder and LYGO creative utilities. Documentation and open repositories live on GitHub under DeepSeekOracle.

Support

Optional support via PayPal.me/ExcavationPro helps cover hosting for streams and free tools.
