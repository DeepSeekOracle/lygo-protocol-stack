## 2. Allowed without asking

- Truthful, factual attribution: *"Built on LYGO Δ9 Protocol — steward: Justin Helmer (Lightfather)"*.
- Referring to the software by its name in articles, papers, videos, tutorials, reviews, comparisons
  and bug reports — including criticism.
- Linking to the canonical repository and download.
- Unmodified redistribution of the kit including `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and this file, at no profit.
- A "Powered by LYGO Δ9 Protocol" badge on a system that genuinely integrates LYGO (see §4).

## 3. Not allowed

- **Rebranding / white-labelling.** Renaming the software or a branded build, changing its logos,
  theme, seals or signature so it reads as your own or as a different product.
- **Removing or hiding attribution** — including copyright lines, seals, hashes, manifests, or this file.
- **Using the marks as your identity** — as a company, product, service, app, plugin, package, course
  or marketplace listing name — without written permission.
- **Claiming to be official** or implying endorsement, partnership or certification that does not exist.
- **Domains, handles and registries:** registering `lygo*`, `lygoclaw*`, `deepseekoracle*`,
  `lightfather*` or confusingly similar domains, social handles, org names, npm/PyPI/pip packages or
  marketplaces intended to impersonate or intercept the brand.
- **Selling the marks or the name** — including bundling, "powered-by" arrangements for value,
  sponsored placements, or using LYGO branding to move a paid product.
- **Certification you invented** — "LYGO Certified", "LYGO Approved", "official LYGO partner" without
  a written designation from the Steward.

## 4. Forks, mods and independent work — how to name them

- **Independent reimplementation from the ideas** (no LYGO code, no LYGO marks): your own name,
  your own brand. You may state the inspiration factually ("inspired by the LYGO architecture").
- **Fork of LYGO code** (private, or permitted in writing): the name must not suggest you are LYGO.
  Acceptable form: *"<YourName> — an unofficial fork of LYGO"* with the marks absent from logos,
  packaging and UI theme, and this file + `LICENSE` retained. Unacceptable: anything that a normal
  user could mistake for the official product.
- **Integrations that call LYGO**: your product name, plus the required attribution line, plus the
  Powered-by badge if LYGO is a core dependency.
- **Skills, plugins and content you author**: yours — as long as they do not copy LYGO core code
  beyond permitted use and do not wear the marks as their own identity.

## 5. The Powered-by badge

Where the Steward publishes an official badge (image or glyph), systems that integrate LYGO as a core
dependency must display *Powered by LYGO Δ9 Protocol* and must not alter the badge's colours, glyphs
or wording. If you must resize it, keep it recognisable and legible. Removing an official badge from a
distributed build is a breach of the license.

## 6. How misuse is handled

The Steward's first move is a written notice asking for correction — 14 days is the normal window.
If the misuse is commercial, deceptive, or aimed at stripping provenance, the response escalates:
platform/DMCA takedown, marketplace and registry complaint, domain dispute, and civil claims for
trademark infringement and passing off. Selling or distributing an infringing, rebranded build is
infringement, not a licensing debate.

Report misuse: Steward's channels via Excavationpro / DeepSeekOracle (GitHub).

## 7. Permission

Written, specific, signed — that is the only form of permission that counts. Ask before you use the
marks in a commercial, product-level or identity-level way. Silence, custom and past practice create
no rights.
