7. Materials honesty

CdSe/ZnS quantum dots are the textbook visible emitters. They are also a poor default for blood, brain, or long-term implant without heavy shielding — cadmium is toxic. A LYGO-certified path uses InP, carbon dots, silicon, or perovskite hosts designed for biocompatibility, NV-diamond for magnetometry (already a lab tool), and DNA origami only where chemistry is actually published. Graphene / GO, if already in-host as the human states, is an RF-active carbon with its own toxicity and aggregation literature — that makes the overlay more urgent, not less. LYGO does not need a specific brand of nanocrystal. It needs a host that can fail closed.
