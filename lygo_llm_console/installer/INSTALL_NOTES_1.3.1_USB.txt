LYGO LOCAL AGENT CONSOLE 1.3.1 - USB CLAW (the stick build)
===========================================================

WHAT JUST HAPPENED
  The sealed 1.3.1 stick build was laid down. It is the SELF-CONTAINED copy:
    python\    its own Python - the stick needs no Python installed on the host
    engine\    its own llama.cpp engine and backends
    portal\, src\, skills\, prompts\, workspace\   the console itself
  It runs from wherever it lands, including the stick itself.

START IT
  Start Menu → "LYGO Agent Stick" (stick ports), or <install>\LYGO_AGENT_STICK.bat
  Console + portal: http://127.0.0.1:9651   Engine: 127.0.0.1:11451
  Stop with LYGO_AGENT_STICK_STOP.bat.

THE BRAINS (not in this installer - a stick has no room for 7-12 GB of weights)
  <install>\models\fetch_models.py --profile coder --yes --dest <install>\models
  fetched from our own vault, pinned to one commit, sha256 verified, nothing else:
    https://huggingface.co/DeepSeekOracle/lygo-console-models
    https://github.com/DeepSeekOracle/lygo-console-models        (mirror)
  Profiles: coder 4.7 GB · embed 274 MB · basic 7.3 GB · full 12.5 GB.
  The console scans <install>\models ("scan_roots" in config\console.json). On the studio PC it
  also finds the vault directly (I:\LYGO_MODELS), so nothing needs fetching there.
  On a stick with ~3 GB free, --profile coder is the one that fits; a bigger stick takes basic/full.

WHAT 1.3.1 CHANGES (measured on the studio PC, same ask)
  A two-word ask generated 106 tokens to show 6 - 94% of the turn was text nobody saw. Now: 2 and 2.
  Every turn records generated vs shown, why they differ, prefill new vs cached, cache hit %, limb
  calls and wall time - /api/health "last_perf", the console log, and the portal. `LYGO_TURNPERF=0`
  restores the previous behaviour exactly.
