LYGO Local Agent Console 1.3.2 — PC LOCAL FULL — everything is inside this one install
=====================================================================================

Nothing to download. The console, the chat engine, the picture engine, the brains, and the image
checkpoint are all in the files you just ran.

WHAT CAME OUT OF THE INSTALLER
------------------------------
  engine\                     the chat engine (llama.cpp + its backends)
  tools\sd-cpu\               the PICTURE engine: stable-diffusion.cpp, CPU build, 45 MB,
                              no CUDA, no card, no admin rights
  models\gemma4-12b.gguf      the brain (text + images + audio)
  models\gemma4-12b-mmproj    its vision/audio projector
  models\qwen2.5-coder-7b     the fast tasker / coder
  models\nomic-embed-text     embeddings for memory search
  models\sd\v1-5-pruned-emaonly.safetensors    the IMAGE checkpoint - SD 1.5, 4.27 GB
  models\sd\LICENSE-CREATIVEML-OPEN-RAIL-M.txt its licence, travelling with it as it must

BECAUSE IT IS ALL THERE, PICTURES WORK IMMEDIATELY
--------------------------------------------------
Boot it (LYGO_LLM_CONSOLE.bat), then ask in plain words:

    draw me a lime green sports car in a futuristic neon city

or open the Image generation module on the web portal:

    https://chatagent.ca/portal/?console=http://127.0.0.1:9642

The console finds the engine and the checkpoint by itself: the packaged engine makes the kit its own
media root, and the checkpoint sits exactly where it looks (models\sd). No config, no env var, no
internet.

FASTER, OPTIONAL — AND WHY IT IS NOT THE DEFAULT HERE
----------------------------------------------------
SDXL Turbo is 4 steps instead of 20, so it is much faster. It is NOT inside this installer because its
licence does not let us redistribute it: Stability AI released SDXL Turbo under a non-commercial
research licence. SD 1.5's licence (CreativeML Open RAIL-M) does allow redistribution with the
licence attached, which is exactly why SD 1.5 is the one that ships. Want Turbo for yourself?

    tools\sd-cpu\FETCH_IMAGE_MODEL.bat          (or the Start-menu entry)

It fetches 6.94 GB once from Stability's own repository, shows you that licence, resumes if the line
drops, checks the byte count, and deletes a file that fails the check rather than keeping a bad one.
Put it in the same models\sd folder and the console will use it as the default.

WHERE YOUR PICTURES GO
----------------------
    <install>\workspace\images\gen-<stamp>.png

with a receipt naming the route (cpu / cuda), the engine, the checkpoint, the steps, the seconds and
the bytes - a claim of "drawn" is something the engine measured, not something the model remembers
meaning to do.

VERIFY WHAT YOU DOWNLOADED
--------------------------
Every installer for this build carries its SHA-256 on the SkillHub catalog
(https://chatagent.ca/lygoskillhub.html) and on our HuggingFace vault
(https://huggingface.co/DeepSeekOracle/lygo-console-builds):

    CertUtil -hashfile LYGO_LLM_CONSOLE_1.3.2_PC_SETUP_FULL.exe SHA256

NOTE: 17 GB of weights cannot ride inside one EXE - Windows cannot load a Setup.exe larger than about
4.2 GB - so this installer ships as SETUP.EXE plus .bin slices beside it. Keep the files together when
you move them; the installer reads the slices itself.
